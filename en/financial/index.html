Financial-Business Advisory

Four practice areas aimed at one goal: helping you reach a healthy, stable financial position — with a clear action plan, not just general advice.

01

Financing & Mortgages

A creative approach to financing solutions, including for clients rejected by mainstream banking. We build customized financing structures for investors, developers and businesses — including solutions for negative credit history.

What's Included

  • Mapping financing needs and defining the optimal structure
  • Connecting to diverse funding sources — bank and non-bank
  • Guidance through the underwriting process with the funding party
  • Solutions for rejected applicants and those with complex credit histories
  • Real estate project financing: acquisition, improvement, investment
  • Mortgage tenders and refinancing advisory

Who It's For

  • Investors seeking optimal leverage for projects
  • Real estate developers on complex projects
  • Clients with negative BDI or troubled credit history
  • Businesses needing a financing structure for growth
  • First-home buyers in non-trivial financial situations
02

Financial Recovery & Guidance

When cash flow becomes complicated and economic decisions lose focus — we help restore order. Deep diagnosis, a clear recovery plan, and hands-on guidance until stability.

What's Included

  • Diagnosing the full financial picture
  • Building a multi-stage recovery plan
  • Ongoing cash flow management
  • Negotiation with creditors and additional financing parties
  • Periodic monitoring and real-time plan adjustments
  • Connecting to new credit sources where needed

Who It's For

  • Businesses with cash-flow or profitability difficulties
  • Families under accumulated financial strain
  • Companies in turnaround
  • Individuals needing a comprehensive financial sort-out
  • Businesses after a dramatic market shift
03

Debt Management with Creditors

Professional representation and negotiation with banks, suppliers, and authorities. Where legal proceedings are required — we work in close cooperation with external attorneys.

What's Included

  • Mapping the debt portfolio and prioritizing by urgency
  • Building a negotiation strategy
  • Direct negotiation with creditors
  • Debt arrangements, payment plans, partial write-offs
  • Legal accompaniment when needed (via external attorneys)
  • Guidance through insolvency, stay of proceedings, liquidation

Who It's For

  • Debtors facing banks, authorities and suppliers
  • Businesses struggling with current payments
  • Families with accumulated debt
  • Situations requiring creative, non-standard solutions
  • Business owners who need an orderly wind-down
04

Business Advisory & Growth Strategy

A top-down view of your business, identifying growth levers, and building a business plan that leads to measurable results — combining financial, strategic, and commercial analysis.

What's Included

  • Comprehensive strategic and business diagnosis
  • Building a detailed business plan
  • Pricing model, revenue goals, profitability targets
  • Guidance on strategic decision-making
  • Connecting to potential investors and partners
  • Periodic monitoring, measurement, and control

Who It's For

  • Business owners aiming for focused growth
  • Companies in formation or pivot
  • Businesses at a strategic turning point
  • Entrepreneurs on new ventures
  • Companies preparing for capital raises
