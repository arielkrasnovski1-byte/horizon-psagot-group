Real Estate & Investments

Three real estate practice areas — in Israel, abroad, and urban renewal. Professional guidance combining analysis, execution, and long-term vision.

01

Real Estate & Investments in Israel

Professional investment paths in Israeli real estate — sourcing, analysis, acquisition, and value-add guidance. Work with private investors and developers on projects of all sizes.

What's Included

  • Sourcing high-quality investment opportunities
  • Financial and legal feasibility analysis
  • Acquisition guidance (with real estate attorneys)
  • Value-add and exit strategy
  • Asset management and incremental improvement
  • Connecting to financing and development partners

Who It's For

  • Private investors interested in income-producing real estate
  • Developers on projects of any size
  • Families aiming to acquire an additional apartment
  • Business owners seeking an alternative to the stock market
  • Purchasing groups
02

International Real Estate Investments

Selected international markets offer high returns alongside geographic diversification of risk. We operate in four key markets, each with its own risk/return profile and tailored guidance approach.

🇮🇹

Italy

A boutique market of land and residential assets. Moderate, stable returns, accumulated long-term value, with options at varying levels of involvement.

🇦🇪

Dubai

A dynamic market in luxury and residential. No income tax on rental yields and no acquisition tax on most assets — attractive to international investors.

🇬🇷

Greece

Relatively accessible entry prices with significant upside potential. An attractive "Golden Visa" program for foreign investors from one million euros.

🇨🇾

Cyprus

A combination of a stable real estate market and meaningful tax benefits for foreign residents. Geographic accessibility and friendly registration processes.

What's Included

  • Selecting the right market and region for the investor profile
  • Legal accompaniment in the target country
  • Building international holding and tax structure
  • Remote asset management
  • Exit and sale planning
  • Assistance with citizenship / Golden Visa where applicable

Who It's For

  • Investors seeking geographic diversification
  • High-net-worth individuals wanting foreign currency yield
  • Investors interested in a visa / second citizenship
  • Developers on international projects
03

Urban Renewal

Demolish-and-rebuild, TAMA 38, and land-sharing transactions — guiding both tenants and developers through every stage of the project, from feasibility to handover of new apartments.

What's Included

  • Financial-planning feasibility study for the project
  • Representing tenants against developers
  • Representing developers against tenants
  • Economic-legal accompaniment from planning to handover
  • Financing and construction solutions
  • Managing process complexity vis-à-vis authorities

Who It's For

  • Tenants in buildings under renewal frameworks
  • Tenant representatives in projects
  • Developers on demolish-rebuild or TAMA 38 projects
  • Landowners considering joint-venture deals
  • Tenant groups at early planning stages
