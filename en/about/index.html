Horizon Psagot Group was founded on the understanding that major economic decisions do not happen in a vacuum — finance, real estate, business, and law are interwoven. We provide a single professional framework that connects the dots and leads to smarter decisions.

Our Story

A financial-business home with an integrated approach

Horizon Psagot Group guides investors, developers, families, and business owners through their most complex economic decisions. Our team brings expertise in financing, investment, entrepreneurship, and law, operating under one philosophy: every decision affects multiple dimensions — and requires a wide view.

Instead of clients running between a mortgage advisor, a CPA, a lawyer, and a real estate agent — and reconciling conflicting insights from each — we offer one unified envelope. The professional outlook is broad, the decision is made with full information, and the recommendation is the product of integrated analysis.

We are not a financial institution and we do not manage client funds. Our role is to accompany, analyze, connect, and advise. Legal work, when needed, is performed in collaboration with external attorneys we have partnered with for years.
