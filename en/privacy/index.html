Privacy Policy

Horizon Psagot Group is committed to protecting the privacy of its visitors and clients.

General

This document explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect your privacy. Use of the site and of Horizon Psagot Group's services constitutes consent to this policy.

What Information We Collect

  • Information you provide voluntarily: name, phone, email, and any details you submitted in the contact form on the site or provided in a conversation.
  • Basic technical information: browser type, operating system, IP address — for security and troubleshooting only.
  • Site interaction: pages visited, time on site and referral source, via Google Analytics (GA4). Data is aggregated and anonymous, and IP addresses are truncated.

Purposes of Information Use

  • Returning a response to your initiated inquiry
  • Improving the quality of services and the site
  • Information security and preventing malicious site use
  • Compliance with legal or regulatory requirements

Sharing Information with Third Parties

We do not transfer your details to commercial third parties. Possible exceptions:

  • Sharing with external attorneys we work with, only if requested to handle a legal matter related to your inquiry — and with your explicit consent.
  • Legal obligation — if required by an authorized authority or a court order.
  • Technical service providers (such as email services, site hosting) — receive only the minimum information needed to operate the service, and are subject to confidentiality obligations.

Information Security

We take accepted security measures, both technical and organizational, to protect your information. However, no internet system is 100% secure, and absolute prevention of information leakage cannot be guaranteed.

Your Rights

In accordance with the Privacy Protection Law (5741-1981), you have the following rights:

  • Receive access to information held about you
  • Request deletion of information from our databases
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Remove from mailing lists and stop receiving initiated communications

Cookies

The site uses Google Analytics cookies for statistical measurement and improving the user experience. We do not use cookies for advertising or ad personalisation. You can block cookies through your browser settings — site functionality will not be significantly affected.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material updates will be published on the site. We recommend reviewing the policy periodically.
