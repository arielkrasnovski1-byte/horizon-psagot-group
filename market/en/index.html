FAQ

Frequently asked

Some of the things people ask us most.

Who is this for?
For anyone who wants to understand stock or crypto investing in an organized way — whether you're just starting out or already investing and want to go deeper and gain a broader view.
I'm a complete beginner — is this for me?
Absolutely. We start from the basics, in plain language, at a pace that works for you. Our knowledge hub is built exactly for explaining the concepts before you make decisions.
Does the content cover stocks, crypto or both?
Both. Horizon Market's advantage is precisely that integrated view — looking at the two worlds together rather than treating each in isolation.
Isn't investing in stocks and crypto risky?
Every investment carries risk, and crypto in particular can be volatile. We don't promise gains and we don't recommend investments — the content is meant to help you understand the risks. Full transparency is a guiding principle for us.
Do you provide investment advice?
No. Horizon Market is an educational content site. Nothing here is investment advice, investment marketing or a recommendation to act, and it does not replace personal advice from a licensed professional. Have a question about the content? Reach us through the form below or on WhatsApp.
